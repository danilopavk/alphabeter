Alphabeter helps toddlers learn their letters and digits, one big letter at a time, played together with a parent.

How it works

  1. A big letter or digit fills the screen.
  2. Your kid says what it is.
  3. You tap Yes or No.

It follows your kid

Every letter keeps a score. The ones your kid knows least come up most often, and the ones they already know come up less, so each round goes where it helps.
