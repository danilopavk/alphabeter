How it works

  1. A big letter or digit fills the screen.
  2. Your kid says what it is.
  3. You tap Yes or No.

It follows your kid

Every letter keeps a score. The ones your kid knows least come up most often, and the ones they already know come up less, so each round goes where it helps.

One new letter at a time

It starts with just a few: {{.Start}}. As your kid gets to know them, new ones join, one at a time, until all 26 letters and 10 digits are in play. Once a letter joins it stays, so the ones they learned early keep coming back.
